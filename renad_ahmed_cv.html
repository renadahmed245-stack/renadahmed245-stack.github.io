About me

I'm a Cybersecurity student at the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, Egyptian Russian University. My studies combine security fundamentals with AI, which gives me a practical view of how modern attacks work and how intelligent systems can stop them.

Alongside my studies, I work as a Web Developer and Data Analyst at Chic Homz. I hold a Machine Learning certificate from Huawei and a Python certificate from the university's internal training, sharpen my skills through hands-on labs on TryHackMe, and have experience in security auditing with Splunk.

I enjoy problem solving, working in teams and learning new tools. I'm currently part of a seven-member team building our graduation project, an AI-powered defensive security agent.
